What does the character say? How?

💭

T · Thoughts

What does the character think or feel inside?

E · Effect on others

How do others react to the character?

🏃

A · Actions

What does the character do?

👀

L · Looks

Appearance and body language

🧭

Trait · Feeling · Motivation

Trait = always · Feeling = now · Motivation = why

📝 Examples

“Mary stamped her foot. ‘I won’t go! You can’t make me!’” → Trait: stubborn / spoiled. Clues: Speech (“You can’t make me”) and Action (stamped her foot).
“Matt glanced at the dark sky, then climbed the ladder anyway, gripping each rung until his knuckles were white.” → Feeling: afraid. Trait: brave. White knuckles show fear (Looks); climbing anyway shows courage (Action).
“Whenever Dickon walked into the garden, the robin flew down to his shoulder and the other children stopped arguing.” → Trait: gentle and calming. Clue: Effect on others; animals and people relax around him.
COLLECT CLUES → INFER A TRAIT → TRACK THE CHANGE → COMPARE
Watch out! A feeling is not a trait. Someone who is angry once isn’t an angry person. A trait shows up again and again in the character’s actions and words.
