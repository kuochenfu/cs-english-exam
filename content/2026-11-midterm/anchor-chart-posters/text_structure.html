Text Structure

How did the author organize the ideas?

🔍

Description

Topic + details · for example, such as

🔢

Sequence / Chronological

Time order or steps · first, next, finally

⚖️

Compare & Contrast

Alike + different · both, however, unlike

💥

Cause & Effect

Why → result · because, as a result

🧩

Problem & Solution

Problem → fix · solve, solution

📚

Chapters · Scenes · Stanzas

Parts that build a story, play, or poem

📝 Examples

“First, soak the bean seeds overnight. Next, plant them two centimeters deep. After about a week, a curled shoot pushes up through the soil.” → Sequence. Steps in time order: first, next, after about a week.
“Both airships and airplanes carry passengers. However, an airship floats because it is filled with a light gas, while an airplane needs wings and speed to stay up.” → Compare & contrast. “Both” shows a likeness; “however” and “while” show differences.
“Because the spring rains came late, the soil stayed dry. As a result, many seeds did not sprout until May.” → Cause & effect. Late rain (cause) → dry soil → late sprouting (effects).
FIND THE TOPIC → SPOT SIGNAL WORDS → SKETCH THE ORGANIZER
Watch out! One “because” does not make a whole passage cause and effect. Choose the structure that organizes MOST of the text.
