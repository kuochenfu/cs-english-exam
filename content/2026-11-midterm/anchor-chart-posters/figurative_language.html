Figurative Language

Words that mean more than they say

🔗

Simile

Compares with LIKE or AS · floated like a silver whale

🪞

Metaphor

Says one thing IS another · the garden was a sleeping giant

🗣️

Personification

Human actions for non-humans · the wind tugged at my sleeve

🎈

Hyperbole

Big exaggeration · I’ve told you a million times!

🧠

Idiom

Special meaning · break a leg = good luck

🔊

Sound devices

Alliteration: silent silver sky · Onomatopoeia: creak, whoosh

📝 Examples

“The clouds swallowed the airship whole.” → Personification / metaphor. Clouds can’t swallow; it means the ship disappeared completely into thick clouds. It makes the clouds seem huge and dangerous.
“The tendrils clung to the wall like tiny green hands.” → Simile (uses like). The curly stems hold on tightly, the way small hands would grip.
“Colin’s room was a prison.” → Metaphor. The room is not a real prison; it shows that Colin feels trapped and lonely there.
NAME IT → EXPLAIN WHAT IT MEANS → TELL ITS EFFECT
Watch out! Not every “like” is a simile: “I like apples” compares nothing. A simile compares two DIFFERENT kinds of things: “Her voice was like a bell.”
