Topic ≠ Theme

Topic: courage · Theme: Courage means acting even when you are afraid.

Challenge

What problem does the character face?

🤔

Response

What choices does the character make?

🔄

Outcome & Change

What happens? How does the character change?

💡

Lesson

What can EVERY reader learn?

📝 Examples

A lonely, angry girl secretly cares for a dying garden. As the plants grow, she makes friends and becomes kind. → Theme: Caring for something outside yourself can help you grow too. Topic: growth, friendship.
A cabin boy is afraid of heights, but when a stranger’s balloon is in trouble, he climbs out to help and saves the man. → Theme: Courage means doing the right thing even when you are afraid.
A boy tries to write a poem for a contest. Each version fails, but he keeps adding lines until it becomes his best work. → Theme: Mistakes and hard work are part of creating something good.
CHALLENGE → RESPONSE → OUTCOME → LESSON FOR EVERYONE
Watch out! Don’t retell the plot (“Mary found a garden”) and don’t write only a topic (“nature”). A theme also isn’t one character’s action; it is a lesson for everyone.
