## Phonics Sort Groupings Used by the App

Short vowels (Week 1):
- Short a: grasp, gravity
- Short e: breath, energy, feather, weather
- Short i: blister, promise
- Short o: solid
- Short u: crush, numb, shovel

Long a / long e (Week 2):
- Long a: ashamed, awake, brain, display, praise, remain
- Long e: beneath, disease, feast, niece, release, thief
- Patterns: a_e (ashamed, awake), ai (brain, praise, remain), ay (display), ea (beneath, disease, feast, release), ie (niece, thief)

Long i / long o (Week 3):
- Long i: brighten, excite, height, mighty, reply, sign, silent
- Long o: compose, control, dough, odor, thrown
- Patterns: igh/eigh (brighten, mighty, height), i_e (excite), i or y (sign, silent, reply), o_e (compose), o (control, odor), ough/ow (dough, thrown)
