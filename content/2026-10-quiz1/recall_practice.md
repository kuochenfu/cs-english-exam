# Quiz 1 recall practice — 2026-09-28

This supplement is authored practice material, not a transcription of additional school questions. The original 30 vocabulary words and 36 spelling words remain unchanged. Original source transcriptions and `raw/` photographs are preserved.

## Learning flow

- Week 1 / 2 / 3 or all weeks; separate Learn, Practice, and Mini test entry points in Vocabulary and Spelling.
- Learn shows word cards and audio; it does not award scores or stars.
- Practice accepts typed answers. Vocabulary hints show a definition, first letter, and a word bank; spelling hints explain the relevant spelling pattern. Hints are optional and are counted separately.
- A wrong answer stays visible until the learner chooses to hide it and retype it. Corrections never raise the first-answer score. The word returns after up to three other queued questions, or sooner at the end of a short round.
- Each failed first attempt receives one delayed recheck. A second failure remains in the missed list for the next practice; an immediate correction alone never clears it.
- Mini tests sample up to 10 words from the selected week(s), have no hints, and reveal answers only at the end.
- Vocabulary matching and multiple-choice modes remain available. Authored cloze questions have exact inflected answers, fixed distractors, explanatory feedback, and a manual Next button.
- Reading, grammar, listening, and other exams retain their existing flows.

## Scoring and saved data

New scores use `vocab:q1-*` / `spell:q1-*` topic IDs, separated by week and mode. New recall misses share `vocab:q1-context` / `spell:q1-recall` by word. The old per-list Type Spelling entry uses its existing missed key but writes first-answer scores to a new `:independent` key. Existing scores, badges, voice choice, and other exams are retained. Existing reset controls clear the new keys because they remain in the exam namespace.

This version reports round-level independent answers, hints, corrections, and rechecks; long-term multidimensional mastery scheduling is not part of this first release. Closing or leaving a round does not save its unfinished score; missed answers already recorded remain available.
