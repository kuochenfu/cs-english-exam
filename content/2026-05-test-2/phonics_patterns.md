## Suffixes: -ful, -less, -ness, -ment

A suffix is a word part that appears after a root word and changes the word's meaning.

Common suffixes:
- **-ful**
- **-less**
- **-ness**
- **-ment**

Examples from the worksheet:
- delightful
- graceful
- wasteful
- fearless
- breathless
- penniless
- clumsiness
- closeness
- weakness
- pavement
- statement
- appointment

## VCCV Pattern

VCCV words have two consonants in the middle. They usually divide between the two consonants.

Words from the worksheet:
- pattern
- perfect
- collect
- picture
- support
- thirty
- traffic
- plastic
- borrow
- fortune
- attend
- seldom

Sort-game grouping used by the app:
- Double consonants: support, borrow, collect, traffic, attend
- Different consonants: thirty, pattern, perfect, fortune, picture, plastic, seldom

## VCV Syllable Division

If a VCV word divides before the consonant, the first syllable is open and has a long vowel sound.
If a VCV word divides after the consonant, the first syllable is closed and has a short vowel sound.

Words from the worksheet:
- mutant
- music
- siren
- motel
- habit
- polite
- relay
- unite
- punish
- figure
- detail
- frozen

Sort-game grouping used by the app:
- Open first syllable: mutant, music, siren, polite, motel, relay, unite, frozen, detail
- Closed first syllable: habit, punish, figure
