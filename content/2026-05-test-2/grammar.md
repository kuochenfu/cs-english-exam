## Lesson 15 — Adjectives

Core ideas:
- Adjectives describe nouns.
- Adjectives can come before nouns.
- Adjectives can follow a **be** verb and describe the subject.

Patterns seen in the workbook:
- Identify adjective + noun.
- Choose an adjective to complete a sentence.
- Write opposite adjectives.
- Change adjectives into adverbs with **-ly** spelling rules.

Useful adjective pairs:
- big / small
- young / old
- hot / cold
- tall / short
- clean / dirty
- wet / dry
- quiet / noisy
- happy / sad
- long / short
- weak / strong
- poor / rich

## Lesson 16 — Adverbs

Core ideas:
- Adverbs tell more about verbs.
- Adverbs usually come after the verb.
- When the verb has an object, adverbs come after the object.

Patterns seen in the workbook:
- Identify the verb and adverb in a sentence.
- Choose the correct form of an adjective or adverb.
- Write sentences in the correct word order.
- Label the adjective(s) and adverb in full sentences.
- Fill in the blank with the correct adjective or adverb form of a root word.

Common adverbs:
- quickly
- slowly
- easily
- loudly
- quietly
- softly
- neatly
- carefully
- noisily
- happily
- simply
- clearly

Spelling rules:
- Most adjectives: add **-ly**.
- Adjectives ending in **-y**: change **y** to **i** and add **-ly**.
- Adjectives ending in consonant + **le**: change final **e** to **y**.
